Syden terms of use

Last updated October 10, 2026

This is a translation of the original in Portuguese. Syden is run from Brazil, and if the two ever differ, the Portuguese version is the one that applies.

Syden is a free voice, video and chat app for groups of friends. By creating an account, you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

1. Access

Anyone can create an account by confirming an email address; joining a community takes an invite from someone who is already in it. The account is personal: don't share your password. You can delete your account whenever you like, under Settings → My account — it disappears from Syden immediately, and a copy of what you wrote is kept for 90 days outside the app, in case an authority requests it. Nobody reads that copy through Syden, not even whoever administers it; after the 90 days, it is deleted on its own. The details are in the privacy policy.

Joining through a class link. Whoever administers a community can create a link that goes straight to a voice room, valid for a few hours. Anyone who joins through it without an account just types a name and gets a temporary account, which only serves that class and is deleted when the link expires — the same way as any deleted account, including the copy kept for 90 days described above. Whoever is teaching can turn the link off earlier.

2. Living together

You may not use Syden for:

  • harassment, threats, hate speech or discrimination;
  • illegal content, including any sexual content involving minors;
  • nudity, explicit sexual content or pornography;
  • real graphic violence — images or videos of injuries, death or cruelty;
  • sharing other people's personal data without permission;
  • spam, scams or malicious software;
  • trying to break into, overload or get around the security of the service.

3. What you upload

You are responsible for what you post: messages, avatar, emojis, sounds and karaoke songs. Only upload content that is yours or that you have the right to use. The content remains yours; you only authorize Syden to store it and show it to the server's members so the app can work.

About music, a warning: having a file is not the same as having the right to share it. Buying a CD or an MP3 gives you a copy to listen to; playing it for other people is a separate right that doesn't come with the purchase. Syden brings no music library at all, and is not a way to get music — each community uploads what its own members already have.

4. If something of yours was posted here without permission

If you own a work — music, image, video, text — and it is on Syden without your authorization, write to direitos@syden.chat. Say what it is, where it is and why the right is yours. You don't need a Syden account for this.

What happens next: the content is taken down, and whoever posted it is told why. If that person says they had the right, they are heard — and while it isn't clear, the content stays down. Doing it again costs the account.

This address is for copyright. For any other matter, including reporting someone's conduct, contato@syden.chat — and inside the app, the fastest way is the report button, in the right-click menu.

5. Moderation

The server's administrator can delete messages, emojis, sounds and channels, and remove members who break these terms. Removing a member deletes their account and their messages.

Anyone can report a message or a member, through the right-click menu. The report keeps a copy of what was reported and goes to whoever looks after Syden, who decides what to do and records the decision. Reporting in bad faith, to bother someone, also breaks these terms.

Every moderation action — deleting another person's message, removing a member, deleting an account, granting a role — is logged with name and time, so that you can ask for an explanation later.

6. The service

Syden is offered free of charge and "as is", by a personal project. We do our best to keep it running, but it may become unavailable, change or be shut down. There is no guarantee of availability and no liability for losses caused by using the service or by its absence, to the extent permitted by law.

7. Privacy

What we store and how is explained in the privacy policy.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Brazil.
