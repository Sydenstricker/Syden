Syden terms of use

Last updated October 25, 2026

This is a translation of the original in Portuguese. Syden is run from Brazil, and if the two ever differ, the Portuguese version is the one that applies.

Syden is a free voice, video and chat app for groups of friends. By creating an account, you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

  • Free and complete: no paid version and no locked features.
  • No ads, at all.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • In 72 languages.

1. Access

Anyone aged 13 or older can create an account, giving their date of birth and confirming an email address (the rules for people under 18 are in section 2); joining a community takes an invite from someone who is already in it. The account is personal: don't share your password. You can delete your account whenever you like, under Settings → My account — it disappears from Syden immediately, and a copy of what you wrote is kept for 90 days outside the app, in case an authority requests it. Nobody reads that copy through Syden, not even whoever administers it; after the 90 days, it is deleted on its own. The details are in the privacy policy.

Joining through a class link. Whoever administers a community can create a link that goes straight to a voice room, valid for a few hours. Anyone who joins through it without an account just types a name and gets a temporary account, which only serves that class and is deleted when the link expires — the same way as any deleted account, including the copy kept for 90 days described above. Whoever is teaching can turn the link off earlier. This account doesn't ask for an age: it can't chat privately, doesn't join any community and only reaches the class room, organized by whoever created it.

2. Age, minors and guardians

Syden is for people aged 13 or older, and was designed to be safe for teenagers too. The rules below follow Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (ECA Digital, Law 15,211/2025).

  • The date of birth is asked of everyone, when creating the account (or on first sign-in, for people who already had one). It is only used to know the age range, nobody sees it, and once given it only changes on request to contato@syden.chat.
  • From 13 to 15, the account is linked to a guardian's — the father, mother or legal guardian, who needs an adult Syden account and declares being the guardian. There are 7 days to do it; after that, the account is paused until it is linked.
  • Under 18, everything starts closed: private chat only with friends; screen and camera are shown only to friends; only friends' streams can be watched; and the preview of a room's streams is off. From 13 to 15, only the guardian can open any of these rules; at 16 and 17, the person decides.
  • The guardian sees the daily usage time, the adults the teenager talks to or is friends with, and the account's rules, and can set a daily time limit. They don't read the messages.

How age is checked, and our commitment. Today, the age is the one the person gives, with the guardian's declaration and the option of reporting someone who seems to have lied (the account is suspended, with the right to appeal). We know this is not enough. That is why Syden commits here to adopting the gov.br age token as soon as the Brazilian government offers it to apps: with it, the person confirms their age range through their gov.br account, and Syden receives only the confirmation — no CPF, no document, no date. It will be used to confirm the age of accounts and of guardians. Until then, in the Windows app, Syden will also use the age range that Microsoft itself reports for the computer's account, when it is available.

3. Living together

You may not use Syden for:

  • harassment, threats, hate speech or discrimination;
  • illegal content, including any sexual content involving minors;
  • nudity, explicit sexual content or pornography;
  • real graphic violence — images or videos of injuries, death or cruelty;
  • sharing other people's personal data without permission;
  • spam, scams or malicious software;
  • trying to break into, overload or get around the security of the service.

4. What you upload

You are responsible for what you post: messages, avatar, emojis, sounds and karaoke songs. Only upload content that is yours or that you have the right to use. The content remains yours; you only authorize Syden to store it and show it to the server's members so the app can work.

About music, a warning: having a file is not the same as having the right to share it. Buying a CD or an MP3 gives you a copy to listen to; playing it for other people is a separate right that doesn't come with the purchase. Syden brings no music library at all, and is not a way to get music — each community uploads what its own members already have.

5. If something of yours was posted here without permission

If you own a work — music, image, video, text — and it is on Syden without your authorization, write to direitos@syden.chat. Say what it is, where it is and why the right is yours. You don't need a Syden account for this.

What happens next: the content is taken down, and whoever posted it is told why. If that person says they had the right, they are heard — and while it isn't clear, the content stays down. Doing it again costs the account.

This address is for copyright. For any other matter, including reporting someone's conduct, contato@syden.chat — and inside the app, the fastest way is to report (section 6).

6. Moderation

The server's administrator can delete messages, emojis, sounds and channels, and remove members who break these terms. Removing a member deletes their account and their messages. In a call, they can also cut someone's screen share and camera without removing them from the room.

Anyone can report: a message (with the flag next to it), a person and a screen share (through the right-click menu on them), and the age of someone who seems to be under 13. A screen-share report carries the image of that moment, which only the Syden team sees. Whoever reports can mark that it involves a risk to a child or teenager, and those reports are looked at first. The report keeps a copy of what was reported and goes to whoever looks after Syden, who decides what to do and records the decision. Reporting in bad faith, to bother someone, also breaks these terms.

Bans and suspensions. The Syden team can ban an account from all of Syden, or suspend it on suspicion of age. The decision is always made by a person, never automatically, and comes with the reason: whoever was banned or suspended sees the reason when opening Syden and can appeal right there, or at contato@syden.chat. A banned account keeps existing, so the same email can't open another one.

Serious cases. Exploitation, abuse or grooming of a child or teenager: the content is taken down immediately, the evidence is kept for as long as the law requires, and the case is reported to the authorities (such as SaferNet Brasil and the Federal Police), with or without a report, as ECA Digital requires.

Every moderation action — deleting another person's message, removing a member, deleting an account, granting a role — is logged with name and time, so that you can ask for an explanation later.

There is no guarantee of availability and no liability for losses caused by using the service or by its absence, to the extent permitted by law.
